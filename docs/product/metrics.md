# Validation metrics

## Initial cohort

Recruit at least 50 real users through acquaintances, Telegram channels/groups, and organic content. At least half should not be close friends of the product owner.

## Continuation gate

The MVP passes its initial validation gate when:

- at least 60% complete income and savings-target onboarding;
- at least 30% remain active in week two;
- active users record an average of at least ten transactions per week;
- at least 20% report that the product helped control spending;
- at least 10% reach the purchase page;
- at least three users make a real payment or genuine pre-purchase commitment.

Membership count alone is not success. If the gate fails, revisit the problem, audience, onboarding, or value proposition before adding features.

## Required events

- onboarding started/completed/skipped by step;
- first transaction previewed/confirmed/corrected;
- savings target created/changed;
- coaching mode selected/changed;
- alert delivered/opened/muted;
- weekly report viewed;
- paywall viewed and checkout started/completed/failed;
- referral link opened and each qualification milestone reached;
- trial expired and subscription renewed/churned;
- export requested and deletion completed.

Analytics must not contain raw transaction descriptions, document contents, credentials, or payment secrets.
